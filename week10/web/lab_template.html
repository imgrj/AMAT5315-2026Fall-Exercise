Approximate Tensor Contraction for the Ising Model

Week 10 Interactive

Truncated SVD, boundary-MPS row absorption and tensor renormalization group — locating the Ising transition from the specific-heat peaks

PART 1 / COMPRESS A MATRIX

What does keeping χ directions cost?

A 512×512 matrix of independent standard normals, seed 2026. Move the rank and read the two costs the sheet asks for: the error against the discarded spectrum, and the storage of the two thin factors against the original 262,144 entries.

The relative error is ‖M − M_χ‖_F / ‖M‖_F and equals the discarded weight exactly; the factors store (m+n)χ entries. Below χ = 256 the factors are smaller than the matrix; 10% error only arrives at χ = 395.

PART 2 / BOUNDARY MPS ON AN OPEN SQUARE

Absorb the rows, watch the bonds grow and come back

Each row is contracted into the boundary MPS, every horizontal bond grows to at most 2χ, the compression returns it to χ, and the row's scale is stored in ℓ. This is the stated case: open 64×64, K = 0.45, χ = 16.

The chain has one core per column; its height is the current right bond dimension. Nothing here is a spin sample — the boundary holds Boltzmann weights that may be negative.

PART 3 / TRG ON A PERIODIC SQUARE

Coarse-grain a million spins in twenty rounds

Two SVD splits and one exact regrouping per round; the tensor count halves, the surviving legs are capped at χ, and N log c goes into ℓ. This is the stated case: periodic 1024×1024, K = 0.44, χ = 16.

The last round closes the network with z = Σ_{i,j} A[i,j,i,j]; setting all four indices equal would define a different network, which is one of the recorded negative controls.
