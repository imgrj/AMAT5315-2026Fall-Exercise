Physical Foundations

Ising Hamiltonian (J = 1)
E(s) = -J ∑⟨ij⟩ sᵢ sⱼ

Adjacent spins prefer parallel alignment (-1 energy). Disagreeing bonds cost +1.

Onsager Exact Transition
Tc = 2 / ln(1 + √2) ≈ 2.26919

1944 exact analytical solution separating the ferromagnetic and paramagnetic phases.

Wolff Cluster Bond Probability
p = 1 - e^(-2/T)

Flips entire correlated domains with acceptance 1, accelerating sampling by >1000×.
