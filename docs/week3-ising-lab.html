Spin and Lattice

A spin is one bit on a torus

Every site of the $64 \times 64$ square lattice holds a spin $s_i \in \{-1, +1\}$, drawn white for up and dark for down. Periodic boundaries wrap edges: right column neighbours left, bottom neighbours top. The whole model is $4096$ bits and a local rule for their interactions.

Energy

The energy counts disagreements

E(s) = -J ∑ s_i s_j (J = 1)

Agreeing nearest neighbours contribute $-1$, disagreeing pairs $+1$. A fully aligned lattice sits at $-2$ per site. Disagreeing bonds carry energy $+1$ and are highlighted red when you hover a spin.

Exact Solution & Criticality

Onsager's Phase Transition

T_c = 2 / ln(1 + √2) ≈ 2.26919

Below $T_c$, spontaneous symmetry breaking leads to long-range ferromagnetic order. Above $T_c$, thermal entropy destroys magnetization. At $T_c$, fractal domains of all lengths coexist.

Cluster Algorithms

Wolff Cluster Flips Beat Critical Slowing Down

p_bond = 1 - e^(-2/T)

Metropolis single-spin updates take hundreds of sweeps to reconfigure large domains at $T_c$ ($z \approx 2.2$). Wolff grows clusters of aligned spins with bond probability $p$ and flips them with acceptance $1$, accelerating independent sampling by over 1000×!
