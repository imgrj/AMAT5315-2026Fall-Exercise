PART 2 / OBSERVATIONS AND POSTERIORS

What do the observations do to the diseases?

Pick a patient case. The bars are exact node marginals from one tensor contraction, with the observed indices capped and every other index summed.

A positive X-ray raises tuberculosis and lung cancer while lowering bronchitis. The disease probabilities move because the evidence mass is no longer one.

PART 3 / CONTRACTION ORDER

Watch the evidence query contract

Each pair step multiplies two tensors and sums the labels nothing else needs. Step through a saved plan and watch the work and the largest tensor grow.

Work adds one multiply-add per entry of the union of the two inputs' labels; the answer is the same for every order.

PART 4 / LOOPY BELIEF PROPAGATION

Convergence is not accuracy

Drag through the recorded sweeps: the residual falls to the stop tolerance while the marginal error settles above zero.

Beliefs use every incoming message; each update leaves the recipient's message out. On a tree this is exact; ASIA has loops.
